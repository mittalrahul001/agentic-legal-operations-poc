01
Overview

One control plane for a messy cross-functional process

Problem

Reorgs arrive as emails, chats, tickets, and documents. The same intent is translated repeatedly across HRIS, workforce planning, Finance, payroll, and identity systems.

Solution

A Super Agent interprets and routes the request. A specialized Reorg/HR workflow validates, plans, obtains approval, executes, and reconciles.

Goals

Faster intake · fewer mismatches · governed execution · complete evidence.

Non-goals

No AI decisions on layoffs, pay, legal conclusions, or business strategy.

Assumptions

Authoritative IDs exist; approver policy is maintained; not every system has an API.

Safety invariant

No system write without a versioned case, valid approval, narrow permission, and read-back.

02
Design assumptions

Illustrative rules to validate with policy owners

These are design assumptions—not universal HR, Finance, or Legal rules. Accountable owners must confirm them by change type, value threshold, business unit, and jurisdiction.

Approval rules

  • HR: every employee or org-structure change.
  • Finance: headcount, budget, cost-center, or GL impact.
  • Legal: RIF/position elimination and cross-country moves; conditional for employment-term or consultation risks.
  • No self-approval for high-risk cases.

Data & systems

  • HRIS owns employee, position, manager, and org data.
  • Planning/ERP owns budget, cost-center, and GL data.
  • Stable IDs exist; some steps have no API.
  • Downstream state supports read-back.

Execution & AI

  • Changes are effective-dated and support dry run.
  • HRIS precedes Planning, Finance, and IAM unless policy differs.
  • AI recommends; it does not set policy or make legal judgments.
  • Ambiguity triggers human clarification.

Runtime journey

Ingest safely
Authenticate the requester, scan attachments, persist the immutable source, and assign one correlation ID.
Interpret and route
The Super Agent emits a typed routing envelope. Low confidence stops for clarification; no write tools are available here.
Build the case
The Reorg/HR Agent resolves names to stable IDs and produces typed before/after changes with provenance.
Validate and authorize
Deterministic rules validate data, derive risk, select HR/Finance/Legal gates, and bind approvals to the case hash.
Compile and execute
A dry-run produces the impact diff and dependency DAG. The workflow engine executes approved steps through narrow connectors or human tasks.
Reconcile and close
Read-back compares observed state with approved intent. Mismatch pauses closure and opens recovery or remediation.

Key technical contracts

  • RoutingEnvelope: domain, intent, risk signals, confidence, specialist.
  • CanonicalCase: versioned scope, before/after changes, provenance.
  • ApprovalDecision: actor, role, case hash, policy version, timestamp.
  • ExecutionStep: dependencies, idempotency key, retry, compensation.
  • ReconciliationResult: expected, observed, evidence, disposition.

Trust boundaries

User content is untrusted. AI has read/propose access. Policy and humans grant authority. Only the connector runtime receives short-lived write credentials.

Core separation: the case database controls workflow state; enterprise systems remain authoritative for business data; the evidence store proves what was requested, approved, executed, and observed.

Finance approval
Budget and cost center move.

06
Trust & resilience

Guardrails around people, data, and side effects

Human-in-the-loop

  • Confirm ambiguous employees and scope.
  • Approve the exact before/after preview.
  • Pause, retry, compensate, or remediate.
  • Enhanced review for RIF and high-risk cases.

Manual / no-API

  • Named owner, due date, and instructions.
  • Minimum PII and required evidence.
  • Attestation is not enough: verify via read-back.
  • Escalate missed SLAs without auto-completion.

Security & PII

  • SSO, MFA, RBAC/ABAC, separation of duties.
  • Redact/tokenize before model calls.
  • Allow-listed tools and short-lived credentials.
  • Encryption, DLP, retention, residency, legal hold.
  • Treat attachments as untrusted input.

Blast radius

  • Dry run by default; canary employees.
  • Per-case and per-batch limits.
  • Reversible steps first; irreversible steps last.
  • Connector circuit breakers and global kill switch.

Recovery

  1. Retry idempotently.
  2. Resume from checkpoint.
  3. Compensate safely.
  4. Roll forward with a remediation case.
  5. Freeze related cases and escalate.

Audit & observability

  • Actor, case hash, policy/model/tool version.
  • Before/after snapshot and approval evidence.
  • Latency, correction, denial, error, retry, and mismatch rates.
  • One correlation ID from intake to read-back.
Consistency model: this is a saga, not a cross-system transaction. “Completed” means observed downstream state matches approved intent—not merely that API calls returned success.

Delivery

Prove safety before expanding autonomy

Prototype

One scenario

Analytics-team transfer, synthetic data, HR + Finance approvals, Legal trigger, mocked connectors, one manual step, failure injection, audit export.

Pilot

One geography

Read-only shadow first, then limited HRIS writes, canary batches, live reconciliation, operations console, incident playbooks.

Production

Expand by evidence

Add jurisdictions and change types only after evaluation, policy ownership, connector certification, recovery drills, and privacy/security sign-off.
